Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Summary

TikTok Publisher runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It has no server, no database and no other users. No data is collected, sold or shared with third parties.

Data the tool accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) to identify the signed-in account, and the ability to upload videos to that account's drafts.
  • Google Drive: read-only access to the owner's folders containing the videos and hashtag files to be uploaded.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and a log of which videos were already uploaded are stored only in local files on the owner's computer. Videos are downloaded temporarily from Google Drive and sent directly to TikTok.

Sharing

Data is only sent to TikTok (to upload the videos) and, optionally, to the owner's own Telegram chat (to deliver the hashtags for each video). Nothing is shared with anyone else.

Deletion

Deleting the tool's local files removes all stored data. Access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok and Google account settings.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project's GitHub issues.
