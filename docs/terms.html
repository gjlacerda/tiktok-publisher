1. About the tool

TikTok Publisher ("the tool") is a personal, non-commercial command-line tool that uploads videos from its owner's Google Drive to the owner's TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered as a service to anyone else.

2. Use

The tool may only be used with TikTok and Google accounts that belong to the person running it, and only for content that person has the right to publish. Use must comply with the TikTok Terms of Service, TikTok Community Guidelines and the Google Terms of Service.

3. Drafts only

The tool sends videos to the TikTok drafts inbox. It does not publish content automatically. Every post is reviewed and published manually by the account owner in the TikTok app.
